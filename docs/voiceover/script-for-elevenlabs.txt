Suresh Iyer. Two thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine rupees. A subscription renewal that failed. The agent builds a causal graph, a D A G. S05  —  bandit and guardrail
starts 1:26   window 23.3s   69 words   372 chars
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Knowing the cause isn't knowing what to do. A contextual bandit picks the action, using Thompson sampling. Plainly: it keeps a probability curve for each option, and draws from it, so it explores while it's unsure and commits once it's confident. It chose to retry on the date his salary lands. Then two gates: R B I retry limits, T R A I quiet hours, and his own consent.
